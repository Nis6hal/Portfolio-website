The Problem & Context

Many university departments manage physical library assets through fragmented spreadsheets or manual paper ledgers. This results in lost inventory, untracked book renewals, and manual calculation of late fines.

UniLib was built to provide an institutional-grade platform: centralizing thousands of titles, automating member check-out/return cycles with barcode scanning readiness, and giving department heads live metrics on book circulation.

System Features

Catalog & Inventory Management

ISBN-based lookups, volume copy tracking, shelf aisle tagging, and automated out-of-stock reservation queues.

Automated Borrow / Return Flow

Streamlined checkout modal calculating automatic due dates, email reminder triggers, and automated overdue penalty calculations.

Overdue & Fine Management

Automatic overdue detection with configurable daily fine calculations, auto-generated fine records, and one-click payment settlement.

Circulation Analytics

Visual reports on most-borrowed topics, active reading trends, student delinquency rates, and inventory audits.

Engineering Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 1: Race Conditions in Last-Copy Book Reservation

When multiple students attempt to reserve the final copy of a popular textbook simultaneously, race conditions can cause negative stock counts. Implemented SQLite atomic transactions with check-then-decrement guard clauses to guarantee ACID compliance.

Challenge 2: Efficient Multi-Field Catalog Indexing

Searching across tens of thousands of books by title, author, ISBN, and tags caused slow table scans. Created SQLite B-tree and full-text search indexes with weighted scoring, reducing query response times from 340ms to under 18ms.
